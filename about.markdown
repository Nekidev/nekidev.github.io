Yes, this blog uses the default Jekyll template.

I'm Nekidev, open source developer of a bunch of projects like [Payphone] and [Nekos API]. I'm also
launching my first company soon, [Duckity], on which I've been working on for the past year and
half. I took too long to launch it, that's one of the big lessons I take out of this.

Talking about lessons, I learn well by explaining the topic to myself when I'm diving into
something new, like C with wlroots to make a Wayland compositor. Surprisingly, there's very little
documentation on how to use wlroots other than Drew Devault's 8-year-old blog posts, so I decided,
like a bazillion other times, that I'd write down notes about it. It works, so now I'm turning it
into a god-knows-how-frequently-updated blog to post my ideas.

Feel free to email me at `rafael@duckity.com` if you have something interesting to say.
